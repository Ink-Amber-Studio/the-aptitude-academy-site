What does The Aptitude Academy do?

We recruit unemployed people, place them into learnerships with registered training providers, and manage the programme’s stipends, costs and completion support. Companies receive one invoice and supporting evidence for their B-BBEE requirements.

How do you work with APT?

APT handles the company’s B-BBEE scorecard and certification. The Aptitude Academy manages the learnership work outsourced to us by APT, including recruitment, placement and programme administration.

Do you provide the training yourselves?

Learners are placed with registered SETA training providers. The Aptitude Academy coordinates recruitment, placement, stipends and programme management.

How can I express interest in a learnership?

Use the learner registration option below to tell us about yourself. You can also contact us by WhatsApp or email to discuss your interest and find out what information is needed.

Does registering guarantee a placement?

No. Registering lets us know you are interested. Placement depends on a suitable programme and its requirements. Contact us to discuss your circumstances.
